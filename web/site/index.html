Questions

Good to know

Do helpers bring cleaning supplies?

No. Helpers use the broom, mop and cleaners in your home. If you are out of something, add supplies to the booking and the helper brings them with the visit.

What if no helper is free in 15 minutes?

The app shows the real arrival time before you pay. If it is longer than you want, schedule the visit for later instead.

Can I get the same helper every time?

Yes. Mark a helper as a favourite and we try them first, or start a monthly plan with one regular helper.

What does it cost to cancel?

Free until 1 hour before the visit. After that a small fee applies, shown in the app before you confirm.

How do I pay?

UPI, wallet, or cash after the job. You get a receipt for every visit.

Which areas do you cover?

We are opening area by area. Enter your address when you book and we will tell you straight away whether we serve it, or let you know when we do.
