Help

For customers and for Saathi partners.

Talk to us

Our phone and email support lines open with the launch.

During a visit, the fastest way is the Help button inside the app. The Emergency button there calls our safety team straight away.

Customers

  • Helper has not arrived: open the booking and tap Help. We find you another helper or refund you.
  • Work was not finished: rate the visit and choose how we should fix it.
  • Something is damaged: report it within 24 hours with photos.
  • Charged wrongly: open the receipt and tap “Something is not right”.

Partners

  • Payout questions: Earnings, then the payout, then Help.
  • Customer not at home: tap “Customer is not at home” on the job. You are paid for the trip.
  • You do not feel safe: use “I do not feel safe” in the app. It calls our safety team.
  • Documents and training: Home, then My documents.
